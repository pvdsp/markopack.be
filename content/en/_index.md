## About Markopack Belux

Markopack Belux is your specialist in permanent marking of components. With years of experience in the industry, we offer high-quality marking systems that ensure optimal product traceability.

### Our Products

We supply Pinstamp and Benchmark systems in various configurations - mounted on toolposts, integrated into production lines, or as portable units. Dot peen marking systems (electric or pneumatic) handle curved surfaces, with single or multiple pin options for rapid marking cycles.

### Why Markopack?

- **Exclusive sales** of Telesis Technologies (A Hitachi Group Company) for Belux
- **Robust and reliable** industrial laser and dot peen marking systems
- **After-sales service** with our own parts inventory near Brussels
- **Complete traceability** with datamatrix codes, serial numbers, and logos

### Applications

Our marking systems are used for marking fixed data, variable information, alphanumeric characters, serial numbers, sequential numbering, dating, datamatrix codes, and logos. Perfect for automotive, aerospace, medical equipment, and many other industries.
